How to Play
  • you earn TT/s passively - no clicking!
  • your main goal each run is to collect every business — keep an eye out for the cheapest one you don't own yet
  • spend TT on Weapons, Trucks and Trailers too — they all add a bit to your income
  • Trailer needs a Truck first, and better Trailers need better Trucks
  • upgrade Skills as you level up — they quietly boost your gear over time
  • check the Collections tab — matching sets of businesses reward you for finishing them
  • the Achievements tab tracks your milestones... and maybe a few secrets as well 👀
  • don't be afraid to Prestige — it resets your run but grants a permanent boost, like a New Game+
  • master a full Prestige run, and something even bigger might be waiting for you
